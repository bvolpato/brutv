You bring the source

BruTV is a player only. It does not provide, host, share, or sell channels, movies, playlists, or subscriptions. Bring a source you are authorized to use.

Pause. Rewind. Go live.

Apple and Android builds offer buffer settings. Supported HLS streams can use a temporary on-device buffer to pause and rewind; behavior depends on format and device.

04
⌘

Move between your devices

Scan a sign-in QR code or use Android TV pairing to set up another device. These actions can transfer credentials you choose to share.

Apple builds include Picture in Picture where supported. Apple TV and Android TV have remote-friendly navigation.
