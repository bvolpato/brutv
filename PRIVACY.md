# Privacy Policy for BruTV

**Last Updated:** October 12, 2026

BruTV ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This policy outlines our commitment to protecting your data.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
- **No Personal Data Collected:** BruTV does not collect, harvest, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to our servers or third-party tracking services.
- **Local Storage Only:** Any credentials entered (server URL, username, password) and user preferences (favorite channels, recent history, playback settings) are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard sandboxed storage (`UserDefaults`).
- **No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising:** BruTV does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or diagnostic telemetry collectors.

### 2. Network Communication
- Network requests are made solely and directly between your device and the IPTV server specified by you.
- We do not operate proxy servers, intermediate gateways, or logging servers.

### 3. Contact
The published policy is at https://brunovolpato.com/brutv/privacy/. For questions, email me@brunovolpato.com.
