# Privacy Policy for BruTV

**Last Updated:** November 8, 2026

BruTV ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This policy outlines our commitment to protecting your data.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
- **No Personal Data Collected:** BruTV does not collect, harvest, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to our servers or third-party tracking services.
- **Local Storage Only:** Credentials and preferences stay in the app's private storage or memory on your device. The storage mechanism depends on the platform.
- **Temporary Live Video:** Supported channels save a bounded, temporary video buffer on your device for pause and rewind. This buffer is removed when you close or change the channel; stale session files are cleaned on a subsequent start. It is not uploaded to us or saved as a recording library.
- **Local Playback Diagnostics:** Apple apps retain up to 10 recent playback-health summaries and one current-session checkpoint on your device. Checkpoints are saved when playback activity changes, without a background reporting timer. An unfinished session is listed as interrupted after restart, using only the last saved counters. This does not identify a crash, freeze, force-quit, or time since that event. History resets when the app version, build, platform, or OS major version changes, so older measurements are not attributed to the current version.
- **Diagnostic Data and Sharing:** Summaries contain numeric startup, player-reported buffering, and recovery measurements, with no channel names, provider URLs, account details, precise timestamps, or device identifiers. They do not detect frozen video frames or general app hangs. You can clear saved and current data in Settings. A diagnostic summary leaves the app only when you choose to share it using the system share sheet; BruTV does not upload it automatically.
- **No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising:** BruTV does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or diagnostic telemetry collectors.

### 2. Network Communication
- Network requests are made solely and directly between your device and the IPTV server specified by you.
- We do not operate proxy servers, intermediate gateways, or logging servers.
- A timeshift playback server, where used, listens only on your own device and serves the temporary buffer to the local player.
